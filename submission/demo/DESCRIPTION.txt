Skyliner: demo video (about 24 seconds)

Skyliner plays real NASA SPHEREx infrared images of one patch of sky in time order, like a short video, so a person can look for objects that move. It is a viewer: it does not detect or name moving objects.

Team FixThisNoMistake. NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026, Challenge 12: Planet X and SPHEREx (Universal Event).
Project link: https://github.com/nacs-970/skyliner
