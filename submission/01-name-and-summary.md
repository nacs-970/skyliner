## Project Name

Skyliner

Team: FixThisNoMistake (https://www.spaceappschallenge.org/2026/find-a-team/fixthisnomistake/). Chosen by the team on 15 Nov 2026.

## High-Level Project Summary

A web page that plays real NASA SPHEREx infrared images of one patch of sky, in time order, like a short video, so a person can look for objects that move. The page shows the real observing times, including the long gaps between SPHEREx's observing windows, one detector (wavelength range) at a time, with zoom, a looping clip and a pixel readout in the archive's own units. You enter a position on a blank page. If it was looked at before it opens at once; if not, a small service fetches the images from NASA's public archive and builds the view while a progress bar shows how far it is, then it stays ready. It is a viewer: it does not detect or name moving objects.
