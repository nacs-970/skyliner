  • Earth science data is the biggest source. NASA Earth science links appear in 7 of 14 challenges and make up 30 of the 138 links. Eleven of them are Jukebox visuals (images and video), not numeric data.
  • Field Shift lists the most data: 26 links. Half come from non-NASA U.S. sources such as USDA, NRCS and USGS. Teams must join NASA data with local soil, crop and weather data.
  • Five challenges point to partner or international data. Dancing with the SARs, Trend Detective, Field Shift, Earth analogs and SPHEREx cite CSA, CONAE, ESA, JAXA, Copernicus, FAO or KNMI sources.
  • Two challenges list no data source. Space Mission Design Game and astronaut health monitoring show only guides, software or mission pages. In Abandoned but not Forgotten, 13 of 14 links are mission pages. The judging rule still says a Global Award needs at least one NASA data set or resource.
  • Hot Spots has one entry point. Its tab lists a single link: NASA FIRMS. Everything else must come from the team.

The path through 48 hours

Figure 2

What happens to data between the Saturday 09:00 start and the Sunday 23:59 close, with the rule that touches data at each step.

  1. 1

    Pick one

    One team, one challenge, one to six people. No work on a challenge before the event.

    Sat 14 Nov, 09:00 local
  2. 2

    Find data

    Start at the challenge's Resources tab. Follow its portals and tools.

    A Global Award needs at least one NASA data set or resource.
  3. 3

    Get it

    Download, call an API or request a cutout. Check size first: one SPHEREx image is about 65 MB.

    Follow each site's data-use terms.
  4. 4

    Work on it

    Clean, analyze, visualize, build. Keep a list of every outside source.

    Outside material must be open or public, with licenses listed. Work is Apache 2.0 by default.
  5. 5

    Disclose SI

    Say where Super Intelligence tools touched images, video, audio, code or data.

    Watermark images and video. Note SI in text and metadata for audio, code and data. Do not use SI to find the idea. No NASA branding on generated content.
  6. 6

    Show and submit

    Demo: 7 slides or fewer, or a video of 30 seconds or less, at a public link.

    The portal takes images only (JPG, PNG, GIF, up to 10 MB). Only the last save is judged. Closes Sun 15 Nov, 23:59 local.

How this was counted

Method and limits

A link is not a data set. The counts are links on the 13 Nov Resources tabs. One link can be a portal that holds thousands of products, and a data set can need several links.

The six kinds are my grouping. I assigned each of the 138 links by its address and title. NASA does not group them this way. Borderline calls: Landsat and MODIS count as NASA Earth science although USGS hosts the files. GEDI counts as NASA although a university hosts it. The NSSDCA home page counts as an archive, but its spacecraft pages count as background.

Hiding guides removes the 57 grey links. Challenges left with no links keep their row so the gap stays visible.

SPHEREx figures use approximate coordinates for Epsilon Indi, RW Aurigae and R Draconis. Image counts were read on 13 Nov and will grow as data is added. The size range multiplies counts by the 64,952,640-byte file I checked, so it is an estimate. See 07-spherex-data-check.md.

Check again on 14 Nov. NASA said resources may be added before the event starts.
