## Quick facts

- **Event:** 2026 NASA Space Apps Challenge, theme **"The Next Frontier"**.
- **When:** Saturday 14 - Sunday 15 November 2026 (48 hours, local time at each event).
- **Where:** 600 Local Events listed on the official site as of 2026-11-08, plus the virtual **Universal Event**. Free to join. Any age and skill level.
- **Challenges:** 14, written by NASA and Space Agency Partner experts. Focus areas: Earth (5), Moon (3), Mars (3), beyond Earth/Moon/Mars (3).
- **Teams:** 1 to 6 people (Universal Event teams need at least 2). One team, one challenge.
- **Submission window:** opens Sat 14 Nov 9:00 AM local, closes Sun 15 Nov 11:59 PM local.
- **Results:** Global Nominees 3 Dec 2026, Global Finalists and Honorable Mentions 17 Dec 2026, Global Winners 26 Jan 2027.
- **Judging:** five criteria, each scored 1 to 5: Impact, Creativity, Validity, Relevance, Presentation. A demo is required (at most 7 slides or a 30-second video), and a Global Award needs at least one NASA data set or resource.
- **Prizes:** No global cash prize is documented. Participants get a certificate on submission. Global Winners are invited to a NASA celebration (2023-2025 winners: NASA HQ or Goddard). 2026 winner recognition is "to be announced". Local events may add their own prizes.
- **Scale so far:** the 600 local-event listings on 2026-11-08 sum to about 115,600 event enrollments (Universal Event included). This is not directly comparable to the official 2025 registered-participant total of 114,094.
- **Thailand:** two official events: Bangkok (virtual, Local Lead page lists a Nonthaburi venue) and Phayao (hybrid, TK Park Phayao). A Chiang Mai listing on a third-party site is unconfirmed (its official page 404s). Details in [01-event-2026.md](01-event-2026.md).
- **Eligibility catch:** the Terms & Conditions bar participation in connection with an affiliation with China or a Chinese-owned entity (including universities). Open licensing applies (Apache 2.0 by default).
- **Defining trait of 2026:** NASA now writes "Super Intelligence (SI)" instead of "AI" on the official FAQ and challenge text, citing a 29 Sep 2026 U.S. Executive Order. Teams may use SI tools but must describe that use in the submission.
