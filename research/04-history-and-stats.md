# 04. History and Statistics

Sources: Wikipedia "NASA International Space Apps Challenge" (compiled from NASA data), official "Space Apps by the Numbers" page (https://www.spaceappschallenge.org/about/results-and-metrics/), official FAQ. Wikipedia flags promotional tone and heavy reliance on NASA primary sources; figures below were cross-checked against official pages where possible.

## Overall growth

- Started in 2012 with about 2,000 people at 25 events in 17 countries.
- Cumulative (official, as of the 2025 results): **487,000+ registrations, 3,400+ local events, 190+ countries and territories, 17 Space Agency Partners**.
- 2025 was the record year: 114,094 registered participants, 18,860 teams, 551 local events, 167 countries and territories, 11,511 projects, 14 agency partners, 124 subject matter experts, 78 Navigators, 617 million social-media reach.
- 2026 so far: the 600 local-event listings sum to about 115,600 event enrollments on 2026-11-08, six days before the event (a different metric from the official registered-participant total; see [01-event-2026.md](01-event-2026.md)).
- Moved under NASA's Earth Science Division in 2017, which added Earth-science challenges. Now run by the Science Mission Directorate (Earth Science Division). Earlier it was under NASA's Office of the Chief Information Officer.
