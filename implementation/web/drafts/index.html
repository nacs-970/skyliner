Skyliner: UI draft

Open the app: a blank page where you enter a position; real SPHEREx images of it open in the viewer (style 1 Studio), and a new position is prepared automatically while a progress bar shows how far it is. It needs the local service for new positions (see the server README) and prepared data for the test targets. The six drafts below use a sample crop and simulated motion.

The chosen style (Studio) for one layout, drawn from the team sketch: a viewer with a toolbar and magnifier inset, transport controls, a video-editing timeline and a collapsible parameters sidebar. The draft is interactive (play, scrub, zoom with a navigator, markers, clip points, display controls). Black is the default theme. It has a dark and light toggle at the top right, and a sidebar toggle (the three-line icon) at the top right of the viewer. Five other styles were tried and removed from the repository on 15 Nov; they remain in the git history.

1 Studio

Chosen style. Graphite panels, one electric-blue accent, Geist. Dense video-editor look; the most familiar and the safest.

Open draft 1 Studio

The viewer shows a real 192 by 192 pixel crop of one SPHEREx DR1 frame (detector D6, 5 May 2025), not a target. Every frame is that same crop plus simulated noise, drift and a simulated moving point, for testing the controls. The timeline windows follow the observed WISE 0855 windows in DR1 (research/challenge-12/data-findings.md); times inside a window are placeholders. The previews above are scaled down: open a draft to see it at full size. Styles share base.css; each draft adds one skin-*.css.
